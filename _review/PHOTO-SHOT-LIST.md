# Photo Shot List — most-deployed facial-recognition locations

Physical locations ranked by how many times the data records a deployment there, most-deployed
first. This is a factual list — street, landmark, frequency, date range — to help plan where to
point a camera. It contains **no composition or framing advice**: angle, light and approach are
Jared's creative call.

How locations were grouped: deployments were clustered by their map coordinates (and the project's
own `location_cluster_id` tags), then within roughly 200 metres treated as "the same place," because
the source data spells the same spot many different ways (e.g. "Croydon North End", "North End,
Croydon", "George St, Croydon"). The "deployments" count is how many separate deployment-days fall in
that cluster across all sources (Met, BTP, private).

A caution on precision: many records only give a street or a borough, not an exact camera position.
Where the data can't pin a physical spot, it says **"needs Jared's local knowledge to pinpoint."**
Nothing below is invented — every street name and note comes straight from the dataset.

## Notes for planning

- **Top tier worth prioritising:** Croydon (entries #1 + #7 are the same town centre — by far the
  most surveilled place in the dataset, and now with permanent static cameras documented),
  Stratford, Oxford Circus, Romford Market. These have both the highest frequency *and* the longest
  histories (back to early 2020), so they carry the strongest "this has been going on for years"
  weight.
- **Most pinpointable (data gives an exact marker):** Tooting Broadway (outside the Tube station),
  East Ham (outside Lidl on High St North), Green Street (junction with Harold Rd), Walworth Rd
  (junction with East St), Barking Station Parade. These need the least local guesswork.
- **Already has 3D documentation:** Walworth Rd (entry #11) — a Gaussian splat exists, so photos
  there can be tied to the existing 3D capture.
- **Vaguest (borough/street only — bring local knowledge):** the "Westminster" cluster (#2), Brixton
  (#17), Ilford (#16), Hackney Mare St (#20), and the Tottenham Court Rd / Leicester Square pair
  (#19). The data gives a street or area but not a precise camera position.
- **A caveat on the counts:** these reflect what's *recorded* in the dataset, which is itself
  uneven — well-documented hotspots (Croydon, Stratford) will be over-represented relative to places
  the Met deployed but disclosed less about. The ranking is "most-documented," which is close to but
  not identical to "most-deployed."

*Source: all 651 deployment records across `met-police-lfr.json`, `btp-lfr.json`,
`private-operators.json` (after the Workstream A merge). Counts and dates are reproducible from those
files.*
